Objectives

  • Mission 1: Launch Day Drop. Get the launch-day console drop to gamers in Ranglore before the midnight launch. 70 km in 60 seconds.
  • Mission 2: Console Convoy. A night run with games consoles, 75 km in 60 seconds. More breakers and fragile cargo.

On your screen

  • Activity Sensing (top left) reads how the truck is moving: smooth driving, slow moving, breaker hit, accident alert, tea break.
  • Shipment integrity is the crate on a pallet at the top right. It drains like a phone battery every time you hit a breaker or another vehicle.
  • IntuTrack 2.0 radar (bottom left) shows your route, the km left to Ranglore, and a ring that fills as you go.
  • Lorries and tempos carry classic truck-art slogans. Get close and they pop up so you can read them.
  • Get stopped under 20 km/h and the police log the challan on IntuDB.

How to drive

  • The truck drives itself. ← → change lanes, ↑ or Space jumps. A D W work too.
  • Stay at 60 km/h or above. Under 60 is one star, under 40 two, under 30 three. Under 20 the police stop you and issue a challan.
  • Jump the yellow-black speed breakers. Each one you hit slows you more and damages the cargo more than the last.
  • Dodge cars, autos, lorries and animals. A glass of cutting chai takes 5 s off the clock and gives you a quick burst of speed.
  • On a phone or tablet, swipe on the road: left or right to change lanes, up or tap to jump.
  • The radio button on the right side of the road cycles through six horn stations inspired by Indian roads, then off. R does the same on a keyboard. P pauses.
